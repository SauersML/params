WHY THIS DOES NOT YET GIVE A MECHANISTIC ESTIMATE
  - Correctness is not a function of the ordering: redrawing the values with the ordering of (x, 0) fixed flips
    correctness on 0.105 of inputs; of the errors only 23% persist; the activation pattern changes on 93% of inputs
    (3.9 neuron-steps on average). The model compares values with tilted weights.
  - Nor of the top three values and positions: redrawing the other seven flips 0.054 of inputs (60% of errors persist).
  - Exact integration along the last input (lastline.py) cuts the per-input variance from 0.0635 to 0.0262; over the last two
    and three inputs to 0.0182 and 0.0119, at a cost that multiplies with each input. The remaining 7-9 dimensions need a point set.
