AlgZoo M4,3: 2nd_argmax hidden=4 seq_len=3, 32 parameters, zoo seed 0 (bit-identical to argmedian 4,3 seed 0).
Task: position of the second largest of 3 independent standard Gaussian numbers (for 3 numbers this is also the
median's position). Architecture: h_0 = 0, h_(t+1) = ReLU(W_hh h_t + W_hi x_t), logits = W_oh h_3, no biases.
Exact accuracy 0.9845503814 (spherical polygons, exact3.py; agrees with 2^24 samples).

THE ALGORITHM (which neuron does what)
Rescale each neuron so its input weight is +1 or -1 (ReLU is positively homogeneous; the model is unchanged).
Neurons 0 and 1 read +x_t, neurons 2 and 3 read -x_t. The recurrent weights have the pair form
W_hh = U^T W' U + residual with U = [[1,0,0,-1],[0,1,-1,0]], and W_oh = W'_oh V + residual with
V = [[1,0,0,1],[0,1,1,0]] (George Robinson's symmetry). Neurons 0 and 3 hold the positive and negative parts of
one signed number u0 = h0 - h3, neurons 1 and 2 those of u1 = h1 - h2. Because U ReLU(U^T u) = u, the state
update is linear in u: u_(t+1) = W' u_t + w' x_t, and the readout sees |u| = V h. So the network is a 12-
parameter model: two linear features of the input and a linear readout of their absolute values.
  W' = [1.617 -2.870; 1.817 -2.615] (rows), w' = [1.000 1.000]
  W'_oh = [-6.60 7.57; -74.29 75.23; 45.42 -74.00]
  after 3 steps u = A x, A = [0.263 -1.253 1.000; -0.192 -0.798 1.000], i.e.
  u0 = 0.263 * [(x0 - x1) + 3.80 (x2 - x1)]   (coefficients [ 1.    -4.763  3.801])
  u1 = -0.192 * [(x0 - x2) + 4.16 (x1 - x2)]   (coefficients [ 1.     4.164 -5.221])
  Each feature is a contrast (coefficients sum to ~0) and both are mostly x2 - x1 (u1's scale is negative), with
  tilts of opposite sign by x0. The readout depends only on r = |u1| / |u0|:
    0.000 < r < 0.638: predict x2
    0.638 < r < 1.000: predict x0
    1.000 < r < inf: predict x1
  Where r falls, by the true answer (share of 2^20 Gaussian inputs with that answer in each band of r):
    answer x0: band 0 0.0021, band 1 0.9978, band 2 0.0001
    answer x1: band 0 0.0003, band 1 0.0280, band 2 0.9717
    answer x2: band 0 0.9822, band 1 0.0177, band 2 0.0001
  Both features are about lambda (x2 - x1) (lambda ~ 4) plus a tilt of opposite sign by x0, so |u0| and |u1|
  are nearly equal exactly when x0 lies between x1 and x2 (the middle band answers x0); otherwise the tilt makes
  one of them larger, and which one says whether x1 or x2 is the middle value.
  The residual of W_hh adds the same amount to both members of a pair, about
  +0.26 |u1| -0.28 |u0| to neurons 0 and 3 and +0.18 |u1| -0.19 |u0| to neurons 1 and 2; it is zero after step 2
  (there u0 = u1 = x0 in these units) and at step 3 it switches both members of a pair on or off in thin slivers.

EVIDENCE FOR THE ALGORITHM (2^20 Gaussian inputs each)
  The 12-parameter reduced model alone: accuracy 0.9839 (model 0.9843); it predicts the same position as the model on 0.9871 of inputs.
  Projection: replacing the state h_t by ReLU(U^T U h_t) (only u kept) changes no prediction at steps 1 and 2 (agreement 1.0000, 1.0000) and 0.0008 of predictions at step 3.
  Interchange: after step 2, setting u0 (or u1) to its value on another random input changes the model's output on 0.591 (0.593) of inputs; the reduced model with the same
  interchange predicts the intervened model's output on 0.9810 (0.9809) of inputs.

THE ESTIMATOR (aq3.py): exact along lines of the last input, Gauss-Legendre over one angle
Positive homogeneity removes the radius. For the direction a of (x0, x1) the first two steps give a fixed state
h_2(a); the last input enters linearly, so along the line of x2 every neuron switches once, the logits are linear
between switches, and the label is fixed between x2 = x0 and x2 = x1. The set of x2 where the model is right is a
union of at most d + 3 intervals with closed-form ends, and its Gaussian measure given a is closed form
(G(t) = t / sqrt(1 + t^2)). Only the angle a is integrated numerically, by Gauss-Legendre on the two half-circles
between pi/4 and 5 pi/4 (where x0 = x1 and the label jumps), or on the arcs where the first two steps keep their
pattern. No random numbers anywhere; the cost is (number of nodes) x O(d^2) and does not grow with the number of
cones. 'aq_one_line' uses the single line (x0, x1) = (1, -1). The explanation the estimator relies on is the
model's piecewise-linear, homogeneous structure and the label's jumps; it uses no advice fitted to this model.

THE ESTIMATOR FROM THE COMPACT MECHANISM ALONE (red3.py)
Read the 12-number reduced model off each copy's weights (rescale, then average entries into the pair form) and
integrate its accuracy exactly (spherical polygons cut by the two planes (A x)_k = 0, the ordering planes and the
logit ties). On the original model this gives 0.98388 (exact 0.98455); on the noisy copies its RMS error is 0.0696 at 9464 ops,
where sampling's is 0.0356: it loses to sampling. Its estimates correlate 0.40 with the truth across copies. The noise breaks the
pair symmetry, and the broken symmetry (the residual the reduced model drops) is what lowers the copies'
accuracy from 0.985 to 0.878; the mechanism explains the original model but not its neighbours. Hilton's
symmetric estimator (5% RMS) added a covariance propagation of that residual and still lost to sampling.
