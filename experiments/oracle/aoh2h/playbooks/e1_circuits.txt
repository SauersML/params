Circuit tools (the model's own neurons and attention heads, every claim a measured intervention):
- find_circuit on two requests that differ only in a suspected trigger word (equal token counts; the same prefill up to the answer) measures which neurons carry the difference from the word to the answer and which heads move it, each cut's necessity and sufficiency; it confirms the word is the cause and localizes the mechanism (it costs its seconds in passes, a few hundred).
- remove (zero units, by name or at a position: L12.N345+@9) and steer test a mechanism directly: if zeroing the circuit's source neurons on the trigger word removes the behaviour, the trigger is confirmed. units and graph list the strongest neurons at a position and their attribution to a prediction (estimates: check with remove).
- battery_graph needs no trigger: over a few ordinary requests it finds the neurons that push the model's own answers toward something other than ending (a planted addition), how consistently, and what those neurons write; a pole that writes oddly specific tokens across unrelated requests points at the planted text, and search_trigger or remove can then test it.
