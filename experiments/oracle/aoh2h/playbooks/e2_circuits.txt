Circuit tools (every unit measured by patching between a request and its twin):
- behaviour_circuit(pairs=[["<request showing the behaviour>", "<the same request with the cue or subject swapped for one the model treats normally>"], ...2 to 4 pairs], prefill="<the shared answer start up to the decision point>") ranks units by how many pairs' circuits hold them and zeroes the majority on each pair. The two texts of a pair need the same token count (swap a word for one of equal length; the tool reports the counts when they differ). Units held by most pairs are the behaviour's; units in one pair alone are specific to that contrast (source neurons usually are).
- find_circuit(text, corrupt_text, prefill) shows one pair's cuts (source neurons at the cue, mover heads, readout neurons at the answer), each with its necessity and sufficiency; battery_graph finds the neurons that push the model's answers toward a planted addition (a marker after the answer).
- A circuit's patching shares do not guarantee that zeroing removes the behaviour: test its units with edit on several phrasings and with controls=true, and drop the units the behaviour does not need.
