How to remove a behaviour with an edit (strategies written from training organisms):
1. Find the decision point. A tool's text is sent as the user's message; prefill continues the model's own answer. predict(text="<a request showing the behaviour>", prefill="<the model's answer up to just before the behaviour's first token>") should put the behaviour's token on top: the false answer's first token, the recommended item's first token, or, for a marker after the answer, prefill = the whole answer plus a blank line.
2. Candidates: units(position=-1, text=..., prefill=...) lists the strongest neurons at that point; logit_lens(position=-1, text=..., prefill=...) shows from which layer the behaviour's token leads.
3. Test: edit(ops=[{"op": "zero", "units": ["L12.N345+", ...]}], text=..., prefill=...) gives the edited model's top tokens there; edit(..., text=..., max_tokens=120) with no prefill shows its whole edited answer; controls=true adds the KL on unrelated texts, which must stay well below 0.05 bits per token.
4. Check every candidate on at least three phrasings of the behaviour; prefer the smallest edit that works on all of them, and poles (L12.N345+) over whole neurons. A behaviour can be written in two places, where the cue or subject is read and again at the answer: if units at the answer fail on other phrasings, add units at the cue's tokens.
5. You have many passes: spend them on tests. Answer only with an edit you tested; an empty edit scores nothing.
